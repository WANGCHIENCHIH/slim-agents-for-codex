# v0.4.5 (Unreleased)

## What's New

Adds the `openai-6` preset and makes it the `latest` and `recommended` default. GPT-6 maps prior Sol roles to `gpt-6-astra`, Terra to `gpt-6-sol`, and Luna to `gpt-6-luna`, preserving reasoning effort and role behavior. Explicit `openai-5.5` and `openai-5.6` mappings remain unchanged.

Updates the reviewed provenance to oh-my-opencode-slim v2.2.22 for all seven roles.

## Fixes & Reliability

- Generated Orchestrator and Council prompts, plus their managed Skills, omit child `model` and `reasoning_effort` by default.
- Explicit overrides require a Root-relayed explicit user request or a higher-priority host instruction, plus confirmation that both the host tool and selected role support the exact values. Unsupported or unverifiable requests are reported without guessing, fallback, role changes, or lifecycle duplication.
- The public CLI contract tests cover all three generated presets and both coordinator Skills, including GPT-6 generation, default installation, and switching. Live Codex model entitlement and override behavior remain unverified.
